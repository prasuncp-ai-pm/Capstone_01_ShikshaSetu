Slide 03 / 07 — AI-Powered Prototype

“The AI magic is not generating the answer.
It is generating the next useful thought.”

User Journey Loop

1. Student submits NCERT Science doubt

↓ ShikshaSetu retrieves textbook context

2. AI asks one prerequisite question

↓ Student reasons & responds

3. AI adapts step-by-step to response

↓ Student discovers conclusion

4. AI validates concept mastery

Pedagogical Guardrails

  • Strict single-turn question: Max 1 question at a time.
  • No premature dumping: Zero direct answers before thought step.
  • Dynamic branch adaptation: Handles correct, partial, or wrong reasoning.
  • Loop termination: Validates once mastery is demonstrated.

MVP Walking Skeleton Scope

IN MVP:

NCERT Class 10 Science, Text chat, Vector retrieval, Conversation memory, Guided validation.

OUT OF SCOPE:

Voice/Image input, broad curriculum, gamification, teacher dashboards, direct payments.

Slide 06 / 07 — Experimentation & Governance

“The MVP is not the finish line.
It is the learning machine.”

Riskiest Assumptions

  1. Do students actually want guided reasoning over fast answers?
  2. Does Socratic guidance teach without abandonment?
  3. Can the LLM avoid answer leaks consistently?
  4. Will parents pay for conceptual growth?

Validation Metrics Hierarchy

  • North Star: Guided Learning Completion Rate
  • Engagement: Socratic Continuation Rate (>3 turns)
  • Quality: Answer-Leak Rate (<5% on Gold Set)
  • Unit Cost: API Cost per Completed Session (<₹0.50)

Responsible AI for Minors

Child Safety & Data Privacy Guardrails
  • Strict session isolation & zero PII storage
  • Automated toxicity & out-of-curriculum content filtering
  • Parental consent & transparent failure fallback states
Slide 07 / 07 — Decision & Next Steps

“Prove the learning loop before scaling the business.”

Stage-Gate Pilot Decision Framework

GO

Strong Socratic continuation, verified conceptual learning gain, and positive parent WTP.

ITERATE

Learning value exists but UX friction, context retrieval, or session cost requires tuning.

PIVOT

Problem confirmed, but real-time Socratic chat creates unsustainable student drop-off.

STOP

Students reject guided thinking, or safety/cost thresholds fail to meet guardrails.

“I am not trying to prove that AI can answer a Science question.
I am trying to prove that AI can help a student learn to answer it.”
