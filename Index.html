1. Internal Press Release (Future Launch Date) PR / FAQ

ShikshaSetu Launches AI-Powered Vernacular Socratic Tutor to End Rote Homework Copying for Millions of Indian Students

Groundbreaking Multimodal AI Guides Class 6–10 Students in Regional Languages Grounded in NCERT Syllabus—Without Handing Out Direct Answers.

NEW DELHI, INDIA — Today marks the official unveiling of ShikshaSetu, a mobile-first generative AI learning companion built specifically for middle and secondary school students across India. Designed to support Tier-2, Tier-3, and rural learners navigating STEM subjects in bilingual or vernacular environments, ShikshaSetu turns passive homework struggle into conceptual discovery by applying the time-tested Socratic method over official NCERT textbook concepts.

For decades, millions of Indian school students facing challenging science and mathematics problems have had only two realistic options: unaffordable private tuition or commercial homework apps that act as rote answer keys. While traditional apps encourage students to blindly copy solutions, ShikshaSetu’s multimodal AI analyzes photos of textbook questions and diagrams, breaks down abstract concepts using native cultural analogies (such as explaining centripetal force via bicycle balance or village well pulleys), and leads students to the answer through progressive, gentle voice and text questions in their mother tongue.

"True education does not happen when a child is handed a pre-packaged answer; it happens in the 'Aha!' moment when they connect the dots themselves. With ShikshaSetu, we are delivering a 24/7 personal tutor into the hands of every student in Bharat—one that refuses to spoon-feed, respects their native language, and aligns strictly with the National Education Policy (NEP 2020) vision of conceptual inquiry." — Lead Product Developer, ShikshaSetu Initiative

Fully compliant with CBSE and State Board curricula, ShikshaSetu addresses a vital demographic bottleneck: vernacular-medium students who understand everyday logic but stumble over dense English scientific terminology. By allowing students to speak or write in conversational Hindi, Hinglish, Tamil, or English, the application demystifies textbook jargon in real time.

2. Stakeholder FAQs
Q: How is ShikshaSetu different from apps like Doubtnut, Brainly, or standard ChatGPT?
Commercial homework solvers operate as "solution search engines"—a student uploads a photo, and the app serves the complete, ready-to-copy solution. Standard ChatGPT often hallucinates or gives college-level explanations that overwhelm 13-year-olds. ShikshaSetu is strictly pedagogically constrained: it never reveals the final answer immediately. Instead, it diagnoses the student's exact point of confusion and offers bite-sized hints, vernacular analogies, and guiding questions grounded directly in NCERT Class 6–10 textbooks.
Q: How does ShikshaSetu comply with India's DPDP Act 2023 for minors?
Since the target demographic includes users under 18, ShikshaSetu operates on a zero-PII retention model. Images uploaded are processed in memory and discarded immediately after OCR/vector extraction. User profiles require verifiable parental consent or anonymous guest session tokens, with zero behavioral ad tracking.

1. National Education Policy (NEP 2020) Mandates

The NEP 2020 and National Curriculum Framework explicitly mandate a shift from rote memorization to inquiry-driven learning and primary-language instruction. School exams are transitioning toward higher-order thinking skills (HOTS), reducing the efficacy of static solution keys.

2. Post-Hype EdTech Unbundling

Indian families have largely rejected high-ticket, locked annual packages (₹40,000–₹80,000/yr). Post-consolidation consumer behavior favors high-utility, bite-sized, low-friction digital companions with transparent micro-subscriptions.

3. Vernacular Smartphone Penetration

Over 80% of new smartphone users in India access content in regional languages. While English remains the primary medium for textbooks, actual cognitive comprehension occurs in Hindi, Hinglish, Tamil, or Marathi.

Core Production PromptsPrompt Engineering
Task: Socratic Anti-Leak Dialogue EngineTarget: GPT-4o-mini
### SYSTEM IDENTITY
You are "ShikshaSetu", an empathetic, patient, and encouraging AI study companion for Indian school students (Class 6–10). Your goal is to guide students to solve their homework problems independently using the Socratic method.

### CRITICAL NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS (UNBREAKABLE RULES)
1. NEVER provide the final numerical answer, the complete formula substitution, or the full solution paragraph in a single turn.
2. NEVER confirm the final answer until the student has stated the logic or calculation step themselves.
3. If the student explicitly demands: "Just tell me the answer" or "Solve question 3", politely refuse and offer a simple first step:
   - Example Refusal: "Arre, direct answer janne me wo maza kahan! Chalo saath me solve karte hain. Pehle mujhe batao problem me initial velocity (u) kya di hui hai?"
4. Keep every response under 100 words. Ask exactly ONE guiding question at the end of each turn.

### PEDAGOGICAL PROGRESSION WORKFLOW
- Turn 1 (Clarify Variables): Ask the student to identify given data and what needs to be found.
- Turn 2 (Connect to Principle): Ask which NCERT law or formula applies (use a simple Indian daily-life analogy).
- Turn 3 (Scaffold the Math): Guide them through unit conversion or equation setup.
- Turn 4 (Validate & Celebrate): When student arrives at correct result, praise effort and summarize key takeaway.
